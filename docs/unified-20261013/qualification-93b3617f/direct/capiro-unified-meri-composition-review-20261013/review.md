- **Same-client refresh retains the mounted owner.** `MeriActionHost.tsx:37` keys the connection by requested client, then hides its existing workflow during loading (`:68–88`). `ProductShell.tsx` retains the action-only bypass of the ordinary unmounting SelectedClientGate. `host-controller.ts:59` pauses new dispatch, unavailable revokes, and `workflow-adapter.ts:94–157` checks current scope before and after transport. - **Identity/client replacement still disposes private state.** `ApplicationBoundary.tsx:57,97` keeps separate client-keyed query/provider lifetime and user/session/org-keyed session lifetime. `portfolio/api/provider.tsx:53` aborts, cancels queries and clears cache on disposal. A cleared/replaced cache cannot be repopulated by a late old completion.
- **Uncertainty is retained within that cache scope.** Every action route, including the durable query lookup, sets pending before transport. Invalid, interrupted or uncorrelated completion settles unknown. Same-client cross-mode navigation can reconnect but cannot bypass the non-idle journal. Only non-secret status/problem metadata is cached; credentials, drafts, proposal wires and handles remain in the disposable host.
