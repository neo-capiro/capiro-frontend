## Limits

Source review does not establish browser, native backend, live API, Linux, image or deployment success. The Objective review excludes a complete association-write workflow; the Plan command journal is scoped to its mounted owner. Historical source reviews remain in Git history and the external retained evidence. Final build and behavioral receipts are separate.
