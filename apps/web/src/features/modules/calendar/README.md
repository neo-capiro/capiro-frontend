# Master calendar

Responsibilities: Calendar owns range/view/scope selection and source composition. Shared Schedule-X owns Month and Week rendering. Existing Engagement operations own caller-visible meetings; `shared/meetings` owns the reused meeting detail and creation workflows. Planner, public-hearing and Federal Register operations retain their original backend permissions and pagination.

Figma targets: Master Month `411:47345`, Week `411:63168`, Agenda `460:90798`. High-fidelity contexts and screenshots were captured before implementation. Exact Calendar control SVGs were exported read-only from the supplied Figma nodes; source IDs, geometry and hashes are recorded in `public/design/modules/calendar/manifest.json`. The shared header/navigation assets remain owned by ModuleShell.

The workbook Master Calendar addendum MCAL-001–015 governs integration. The 31 October hearing requirement extends Master Month/Week to show paged federal and Florida public hearings alongside caller-accessible meetings. Client-scoped views remain caller-accessible meetings only. Master Agenda composes meetings, paged public hearings, all relevant Planner task pages and Federal Register comment deadlines. Government effective dates now consume the published Core PR2403 publisher-metadata contract through the fixed gateway. Date-only values, exact evidence links, revision history, freshness, excluded candidates and always-incomplete local coverage remain visible; forecasts, full legal Dates text and separately issued corrections remain unavailable. Source counts are derived from loaded authorized rows, never Figma examples.

Meetings use end-exclusive timestamp ranges in America/New_York. Hearing feeds use inclusive official calendar days. Month/Week hearing cards retain the announced start text on their official day without inventing an end time. The shared hearing-feed loader retains successful pages and reports stalled, failed or bounded pagination. The existing coverage endpoint reports Florida source checks only; an empty report is unavailable coverage, and federal completeness is not inferred from it. Planner dates remain date-only with their source timezone/time metadata retained. Federal Register comments use the shared cached comment-deadline range loader and the displayed inclusive civil-day bounds, including candidate pages with no accepted dates. Cache-only incomplete coverage, excluded candidates, read time, raw publisher value uncertainty and the latest ingestion attempt remain visible. Comments are distinct from effective dates, which use their own inclusive civil-date range and evidence. The old next-30-days operation remains available to its separate callers. Bounded pagination guards report incomplete sources rather than implying a complete calendar.

Verification covers civil dates and DST transitions, Monday-based Month/Week ranges, end exclusivity, source identity, deduplication and paged reads; interactive Month/Week/Agenda, scope changes, date navigation, filters and meeting dialogs belong in the explicit local browser harness. Live session/provider checks and browser visual acceptance remain separate gates.

Successor scope/agenda repair (MS02–MS04): the shared SelectedClient boundary verifies the URL hint before Calendar mounts. The Calendar picker navigates through that same boundary, and the verified client supplies the request scope and current label. Master Agenda retains successful sources when meeting reads fail, labels the meeting feed unavailable, and avoids complete-empty claims when source coverage is partial. Planner start and due dates produce separately keyed occurrences unless both date and time match, in which case one occurrence names both semantics. These are Modules route facets, not acceptance of the separate Portfolio Calendar ledger or native ACL behavior.

Calendar `date` (validated ISO civil date) and `view` (Month/Week/Agenda) are URL presentation state. View/date controls replace the current history entry; client selection pushes a new entry while retaining those fields. The shared client boundary may then remount safely without resetting the chosen range or view. Invalid URL dates fall back to the current Eastern civil date before range construction.
